Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your CitiFix account at any time. Account deletion permanently removes your personal information from our systems and anonymizes any civic issues reported by you.

1. What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

  • Your account is permanently removed from Firebase Authentication.
  • All personal information such as name, email address, phone number, and user ID is deleted.
  • Your user profile and activity data (points, badges, leaderboard entries) are removed.
  • Civic issues reported by you are retained only in an anonymized form.
  • All links between reported issues and your identity are permanently removed.

2. What Data May Be Retained (Anonymized)

To support transparency, public accountability, and civic analytics, the following information related to reported issues may be retained after account deletion:

  • Issue category and description
  • Issue location (represents the location of the civic issue, not your address)
  • Uploaded images related to the issue
  • Issue status, resolution timeline, and authority responses

All retained issue data is fully anonymized and cannot be linked back to you.

4. After Deletion

  • You will no longer be able to access your CitiFix account.
  • Deleted personal data cannot be recovered.
  • Anonymized civic issue data may continue to appear in public feeds or analytics.

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or data handling, feel free to contact us. We are happy to help.
