Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your CitiFix account at any time. This permanently removes your personal data from our systems.

1. Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • User ID and authentication record
  • User profile and activity data

2. Data That May Be Retained (Anonymized)

  • Reported civic issues (with all user identifiers removed)
  • Issue descriptions, categories, and status
  • Issue location (represents issue location, not user address)
  • Uploaded images retained only as issue evidence

Retained data cannot be linked back to your identity in any way.

3. How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email from your registered email address to:

Email: citifix2025@gmail.com

Please include the email address used to create your CitiFix account. Your request will be processed within 48 hours.

4. After Deletion

  • You will no longer be able to access your CitiFix account.
  • Deleted personal data cannot be recovered.
  • Anonymized civic issue data may continue to appear in public feeds or analytics.

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or data handling, feel free to contact us. We are happy to help.
